## Primary use case: The Home Barista
"Find all beans that people have used with my machine" — the app is a discovery tool AND a learning tool. Users pick their machine, see what beans others have brewed on it, then drill into specific recipes if they want to try replicating anything.

## Roaster fanboy
"I want to see how other people are brewing beans from my favorite roaster". Curious to know what machines are used, how recipes differ by machine, and which beans are most used.

## Single Origin Snob
"I only drink Ethiopia single origin coffee, don't waste my time with anything else"
